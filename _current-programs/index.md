# LU Programs Around the Country

Learning Unlimited programs have run at over 50 universities and schools across over a dozen states. Click on a program near you for more information!

## Current Programs

The dynamic program listing with interactive map has been moved to the main organization website. Programs are organized by region:

- **The Northeast** - Including programs at MIT, Harvard, Brandeis, Clark, Columbia, Cornell, and others
- **The Midwest** - Including programs at major universities in the region
- **The Mid-Atlantic** - Including programs in Pennsylvania, Maryland, and surrounding areas
- **The South** - Including programs across the Southern United States
- **The West** - Including programs at Stanford and other Western universities

Each program has:
- A dedicated website with program information
- Social media presence (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
- Contact information for questions

For the most up-to-date list of current programs and their schedules, please visit the [Learning Unlimited homepage](/) or contact [info@learningu.org](mailto:info@learningu.org).
